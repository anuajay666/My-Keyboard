Only what real users wrote, paraphrased, with the page it came from. Samples are small, so treat these as themes, not rankings. No Reddit threads or G2 reviews turned up (G2 has 0 reviews). The developer's own blog posts at clevertype.co are marketing, so they are not counted as user feedback.

## What users like (keep these)

- AI grammar fixing is the most praised feature (18 mentions on MWM), then faster typing (12 mentions).
- Easy to use, responsive, good themes and customisation, reliable autocorrect ([Chrome-stats](https://chrome-stats.com/d/com.clevertype.ai.keyboard/reviews), Sep 2026).
- Helps people who write too bluntly (tone change) and bloggers who polish posts ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/clevertype-ai-keyboard/id6560110828?see-all=reviews&platform=iphone)).
- A user who left Grammarly says it gives similar quality for less money ([MWM](https://mwm.ai/apps/clevertype-ai-keyboard/6560110828)).

## Feature requests seen

- Uzbek in translation (Dec 2025, [MWM](https://mwm.ai/apps/clevertype-ai-keyboard/6560110828)). Shows demand for more translation languages.
- Move the keyboard upward (see above).

## Gaps in this research

- Only about 20 individual reviews were readable in total. The Play Store shows 3 without scrolling. A proper export of Play and App Store reviews would give counts for each theme.
- No Reddit or forum threads were found with these searches.
- Review text is paraphrased from page summaries, so wording may differ slightly from the originals.

## What it means for our positioning

Free and fully unlocked, text never leaves the phone, no account, and long text is never cut. Those four directly answer the loudest complaints.
